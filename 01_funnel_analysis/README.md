# Project 1 — Olist Seller Acquisition Funnel Analysis

## Business Problem

Olist wants to understand how effectively potential sellers move through its seller acquisition funnel.

The analysis focuses on identifying where leads are being lost and which acquisition sources perform better or worse.

## Objective

- Measure the seller acquisition funnel
- Calculate conversion from Marketing Qualified Leads (MQLs) to closed deals
- Identify major funnel drop-off points
- Compare conversion performance across acquisition origins
- Analyze how the sales cycle varies over time

## Dataset

This project uses the **Olist Marketing Funnel Dataset**, which contains two files:

- `olist_marketing_qualified_leads_dataset.csv`
- `olist_closed_deals_dataset.csv`

The two datasets are connected using `mql_id`.

## Key Findings

- The dataset contains **8,000 MQLs** and **842 closed deals**.
- Overall MQL-to-closed-deal conversion was **10.53%**.
- The largest opportunity is not simply generating more leads, but improving **middle- and lower-funnel conversion**.
- Conversion performance varies across **acquisition origins**.
- Funnel conversion changed over time, increasing from **0.84% in July 2017** to **14.49% in February 2018**, before reaching **9.98% in May 2018**.
- The average sales cycle was **48.44 days**.
- Conversion performance also varied across landing pages and combinations of acquisition origin and landing page.

## Business Insight

The analysis suggests that Olist should focus on **improving conversion through the middle and lower stages of the seller acquisition funnel**, rather than relying only on increasing lead volume.

Acquisition sources with stronger conversion should be prioritized, while weaker sources can be investigated to understand where leads are being lost.

The analysis identifies **where performance differs**, but does not prove the exact reasons for those differences.
